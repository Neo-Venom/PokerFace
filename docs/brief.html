1. Team Poker Face: RAKSHA, a privacy firewall that reads what a field contains, not what it is called

Track 3 Secret Wards · Members: [ADD MEMBER NAMES] · github.com/suyashkrishangarg/PokerFace · offline, CPU-only, no LLMs or pretrained models

Pitch: every field is judged by two independent signals, its label and its values, so unseen headers, ID formats and name pools still resolve; PII found anywhere is harvested and swept from every other channel, and one patient keeps one keyed pseudonym across English, Devanagari and Hinglish without merging different people.

3. How each part works

  • Ingestion: files are hashed and sniffed by bytes (JSON behind .csv is parsed). PDF/JPEG/XLSX/ZIP, XML with DTDs, truncated or NaN JSON, JSON deeper than 500, invalid UTF-8, free-text lines over 64 KiB and unsafe file names (; $( ..) are quarantined; stray NUL bytes are blanked; ragged rows are repaired before profiling.
  • Detection (3 scripts): a clinical shield protects doctors, doses (1-0-1), labs, eponyms, wards, K/C/O and C/O <complaint>, with a lexicon learned at run time. Shape detectors catch any hospital ID, phones in any grouping, Aadhaar/ABHA/PAN and cue-less PINs. Context NER finds unlabelled people (Pt X (62M), unke pati X ne, पिता: X, honorifics, mixed-script names). Pass-1 finds are swept from all channels after decoding %XX, entities, \uXXXX, base64, homoglyphs and zero-width tricks.
  • Linkage & pseudonyms: Devanagari is romanised and folded; multi-key blocking feeds IDF-weighted Fellegi–Sunter scores; clusters merge by descending score under hard vetoes (sex, DOB, Aadhaar/ABHA/PAN, two IDs of one hospital system, conflicting names or relatives). Spelling or corrupted-age conflicts are waived only when two independent IDs agree; a record that fits two people equally stays null. PSNs are HKDF-derived HMAC, stable per secret.
  • Privacy layer: a per-patient HMAC date shift (30–180 days) keeps every stay and gap exact; truthful age bands; tehsil/PIN coarsened by cohort; rare diagnoses rolled up; impossible ages become [AGE].

4. USP

  • Header-agnostic: labels and values are both evidence, so holder, abode, रोगी or a remarks column of "C/O fever" are classified correctly; no practice names or hospital codes in the code.
  • Cross-script identity with safety rails: मोहम्मद ≈ Md. ≈ Mohammad and नायर ≈ Nair link; homonyms, twins, a father and son with one name and phone, and spouses sharing a phone stay apart (0 false merges on every set).
  • Tested on what we did not tune on: a seeded adversarial generator with a held-out name/header pool and a hard level of hostile-export traps, scored with the toolkit's own checkers.

Before the generalisation work the same code scored 55.7 on Gauntlet A (F0.5 0.02, 36 direct IDs surviving) while scoring 98.5 on practice data; now Gauntlet A scores 99.5 and Chaos s101 98.4. Same secret twice gives byte-identical output; a new secret shares 0 pseudonyms.

Choices and cost. Per-patient shifts keep stays exact while a global shift would fall to A5. Age bands are 5 years, widened only for small cohorts (truthful, 85+ top-coded). Districts are kept (public, research value); house, locality, landmark and village are removed; tehsil and PIN coarsen by cohort. Residual risk, honestly: inside the small practice release every patient is still unique on (age band, district, admission week, diagnosis), 30/48 even on (age band, sex, district). The defence is that released weeks are shifted per patient (only 9/48 coincide with any true week) and the attacker's register is 4–5x larger; A4 stays at about 13% of the raw risk.

6. Security design ("assume the server is breached")

  • Nothing raw or intermediate is written: parsing is in memory, the input is read-only, quarantined originals are not copied (optional AES-256-GCM vault, AAD = file hash).
  • RAKSHA_SECRET is HKDF-split into pseudonym, shift, file-name and vault keys, removed from the environment and never written; different secrets give unlinkable releases.
  • Logs carry counts only; the exception hook strips PII; nothing goes to TMP/HOME. An egress gate re-scans every output, file name, report and records (base64-decoded) before exit; CSV formulas are neutralised (OWASP).

7. Known limitations

  • Names written only in other Indic scripts (Tamil, Bengali) rely on context cues and structured-field harvest; no script-specific transliteration.
  • English-origin names transliterate imperfectly (मैथ्यू ≠ Mathew): such records link only through a shared ID.
  • Within a small release, quasi-identifier uniqueness stays high (above); wider bands for singleton cohorts are a policy knob (config/policy.yaml).
  • Attack numbers come from our implementation of ATTACKS.md; run times are from a 12-core laptop (the 2-CPU sandbox is slower but far inside 15 minutes).
